🚀 Key Features & Pipeline

1.	Data Ingestion: Efficiently processed 1,470 records from the IBM HR Analytics dataset. 
2.	Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):
o	Determined the global Attrition Rate (~16.12%). 
o	Mapped correlations between income, department, and turnover. 
3.	Feature Engineering:
o	Categorical Encoding for non-numeric data. 
o	Creation of custom Engagement & Performance Indicators. 
4.	Advanced Modeling:
o	Classification: Attrition Risk Prediction. 
o	Clustering: Professional Segmentation using K-Means.

📈 Initial Key Insights
Our analysis has already uncovered critical trends:
•	💸 The Salary Factor: Employees who left had a significantly lower Monthly Income than their peers. 
•	🏢 Department Risk: The Sales and Research & Development departments exhibit the highest turnover volatility. 
•	📉 Performance Correlation: Early data suggests a link between lower performance ratings and increased attrition risk.
